━━━━━━━━ 1. PRODUCT ━━━━━━━━
Name (decided): "Erebuni 782" — hy: Էրեբունի 782, ru: Эребуни 782, en: Erebuni 782.
The 782 refers to 782 BC: king Argishti I of Urartu founded the Erebuni
fortress (Arin-Berd hill) that year — the conventional founding date of
Yerevan, verified by the cuneiform foundation inscription found in
excavations.

Trilingual (Armenian hy / Russian ru / English en) educational + field-research
app about the Urartian fortresses Erebuni and Teishebaini (Karmir Blur).
Timeline covered: ~860 BC (rise of Urartu) → 782 (Erebuni founded) → reign of
Sarduri II → ~590 BC (fall of Teishebaini, end of Urartu) → Achaemenid /
Hellenistic aftermath → 20th-century excavations.

Two user modes:
• "I am a tourist" (default): fortress guide, timeline, offline mini-wikipedia
  (kings Argishti I / Sarduri II / Rusa, gods Haldi / Teisheba / Shivini,
  daily life, cuneiform basics), e-book library with excavation scholarship
  (Pokrovsky, Piotrovsky and other participants of the Erebuni / Karmir Blur
  excavations) — ONLY public-domain or licensed texts; every book carries
  source/license metadata. Reader: bookmarks, font size, night mode.
• "I am an employee" (PIN/password gate, EncryptedSharedPreferences):
  registry of stones/artifacts built into modern private houses that cannot
  yet be transferred to museums but must eventually reach them and be studied.
  Record: id, category (stone block / masonry / pottery / other), description,
  photos, geo coords + address, discovery date, owner/custodian, custody status
  (in-situ → agreed-for-transfer → in-transit → handed-to-museum → researched),
  next inspection date (WorkManager reminders), audit-log of changes.
  Export/import: full signed JSON dump + photos zip for PC; Android↔Android
  Bluetooth sync (RFCOMM or BLE, last-write-wins + version vectors, conflict
  list; Wi-Fi Direct fallback). No cloud. Offline-first.

━━━━━━━━ 2. DESIGN SYSTEM ━━━━━━━━
Three switchable historical skins — full design-system swap, not color-only:
1) Pre-Urartu (early Iron Age, ceramics, rough textures)
2) Urartu (cuneiform motifs, basalt/clay palette, ornament borders from
   Erebuni wall paintings) — the flagship/default skin
3) Post-Urartu (Achaemenid/Hellenistic layer)
Each skin = colors + typography pairing + decorative assets + icon variants.
Armenian-capable fonts bundled (Noto Sans/Serif Armenian). Layouts must
survive +40% text length in hy/ru.
